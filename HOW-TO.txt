Let's Shop0220 — how to update the shop

PRODUCTS
The product file is products.txt. One product per line:

Category | Product name | Link | Photo link (optional)

ADD A PRODUCT
1. Open products.txt.
2. Add one line, for example:
   Jeans | Black straight jeans | https://www.store.com/the-jeans
3. Save. The live site updates on its own in a few minutes.

REMOVE A PRODUCT
Delete that whole line and save.

ADD A CATEGORY
Use a new category name on the line. A Hats line makes a Hats section.
A category with no lines hides itself.

ADD A VIDEO
Videos | Saturday outfit | https://www.tiktok.com/your-video-link
If there are no Videos lines, that section stays hidden.

ADD YOUR OWN PHOTO
Put an image link in the last spot:
Jeans | Black straight jeans | https://www.store.com/the-jeans | https://your-photo-link.jpg

NOTES
- A line that starts with # is a note. The site ignores it.
- A messy line is skipped. It will not break the site.
- Do not put prices in the product file.
- The name on the site stays Let's Shop0220.
- Do not put brand emails in this public folder.
